Hellooo!! Welcome to my website! My name is Hannah, and I love boba! Today, I shall tell you about my top 3 favourite drinks from Coco :D

1. Mango Pomelo Sago Smoothie

The Mango Pomelo Sago Smoothie is my favourite drink from Coco. It's a rich, creamy drink with a taste of mangos and a slightly sour taste of pomelo pieces. One flaw is that this drink is very sweet and rich if you get 100% sugar. We can fix that by getting 50%! Overall best drink ever.

2. Honeydew Jasmine Milk Tea With Tea Jelly

The Honeydew Jasmine Milk Tea With Tea Jelly is also an amazing drink. Last year, Coco had a deal where you could buy a large drink for only $2.50. Because of this, I'd get this drink almost every week. Overall a sweet and rich drink, still tastes great with 50% sugar!

3. Brown Sugar Pearl Taro Milk Tea

I had this drink once this year, and absoloutly loved it. It was sweet and rich, even with 70% sugar, no ice. The pearls were fresh and warm, and perfectly chewy. This drink was absoloutly amazing! I would definately get it again if a deal comes up again.
